刪除 Refocus 帳號

繁體中文English

方法一：在 app 裡刪除（最快）

  1. 打開 Refocus。
  2. 點左上角頭像，或到底部的「設定」。
  3. 點「帳號」→「刪除帳號」，確認後即可刪除。

方法二：寄信申請

如果你已經無法使用 app，可以用你註冊時使用的 Email 寄信給我們，主旨寫「刪除帳號」。

寄信申請刪除帳號

會刪除哪些資料

  • 你的帳號（Email、使用者識別碼、名稱）。
  • 雲端備份的封鎖時段與目標。

我們會在收到申請後 30 天內完成刪除，刪除後無法復原。存在你手機上的資料，可以透過解除安裝 app 刪除。

重要提醒

刪除帳號不會取消你的 Pro 訂閱。請到 Google Play →「付款和訂閱」→「訂閱」取消 Refocus，避免繼續扣款。

Delete your Refocus account

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Refocus.
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top left, or go to Settings at the bottom.
  3. Tap Account → Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can no longer use the app, email us from the address you signed up with, using the subject "Delete my account".

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, user identifier and name).
  • Your schedule and goals backed up in the cloud.

We complete deletion within 30 days of your request. Deleted data cannot be recovered. Data stored on your phone can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Important

Deleting your account does not cancel your Pro subscription. To stop being charged, cancel Refocus in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
