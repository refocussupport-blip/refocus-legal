Refocus 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

繁體中文English

Refocus（以下稱「本 app」）由 王岳仁（以下稱「我們」）開發與營運。本政策說明本 app 會存取、收集、使用與分享哪些資料，以及你可以如何管理這些資料。

1. 我們存取與收集的資料

無障礙服務（畫面內容）

本 app 使用 Android 的無障礙服務（AccessibilityService），用來辨識你是否正在觀看 YouTube Shorts、Instagram Reels 等短影音畫面，並在你設定的時段內自動離開這些畫面。

  • 只在本 app 支援的 app（例如 YouTube、Instagram、Facebook、TikTok 等）開啟時運作。
  • 會讀取畫面的結構資訊，例如元件識別名稱、畫面名稱、按鈕上的文字標籤與無障礙描述，用來判斷目前是否為短影音畫面。
  • 這些資訊只在你的手機上即時處理，判斷完就丟棄，不會儲存、不會上傳到任何伺服器，也不會分享給任何人。我們不會刻意讀取或保存你的訊息內容、密碼或其他輸入的文字。
  • 每次擋下短影音時，本 app 只會在你的手機上記錄「日期」與「被擋下的 app 名稱」，用來顯示統計資料。

使用情形存取權（手機使用時間）

如果你授權「使用情形存取權」，本 app 會讀取你每天的手機使用時間，用來在首頁顯示本週使用量。這些資料只在你的手機上計算與顯示，不會上傳。

你在 app 中輸入的設定

包括語言、你估計每天使用手機的時數、你的目標、封鎖時段。這些資料預設只存在你的手機上。如果你登入帳號，封鎖時段與目標會同步備份到雲端（見下方「帳號」）。

帳號（選填）

你可以選擇用 Google 帳號或 Email 登入。登入時我們會透過 Google Firebase 取得並保存：你的 Email、使用者識別碼，以及使用 Google 登入時提供的名稱。登入後，你的封鎖時段與目標會儲存在 Google Firebase Firestore，讓你換手機時可以還原設定。不登入也可以使用本 app 的所有功能。

付款與訂閱

Pro 訂閱的付款完全由 Google Play 處理。我們只會收到訂閱狀態（例如是否為有效訂閱、方案種類與購買憑證），不會取得你的信用卡或其他付款資訊。

通知

本 app 可能會在無障礙服務被關閉時發送通知提醒你。通知權限不會用來收集任何資料。

2. 我們如何使用資料

  • 辨識並封鎖短影音畫面。
  • 顯示你的使用時間與封鎖統計。
  • 在你登入時備份與還原設定。
  • 確認你的訂閱狀態並提供 Pro 功能。

我們不會將你的資料用於廣告，也不會販售你的資料。本 app 目前沒有加入任何廣告或第三方追蹤工具。

3. 資料分享

我們只會與提供必要服務的廠商分享資料：

  • Google Firebase（帳號登入與雲端備份）
  • Google Play（訂閱付款）

除非法律要求，我們不會將你的資料提供給其他第三方。

4. 資料安全

雲端傳輸的資料皆透過 HTTPS 加密，並設定存取權限，只有你本人的帳號能讀寫自己的資料。

5. 資料保存與刪除

  • 手機上的資料：保存到你解除安裝本 app 或清除 app 資料為止。
  • 雲端資料：保存到你刪除帳號為止。
  • 刪除帳號：你可以在 app 的「設定 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」直接刪除，或依照刪除帳號說明頁寄信申請。我們會在 30 天內刪除你的帳號與雲端上的所有相關資料。
  • 刪除帳號不會自動取消 Google Play 訂閱，請另外到 Google Play 取消。

6. 兒童隱私

本 app 並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計。如果你是未成年人，請在家長或監護人同意下使用本 app。若我們發現在未經同意的情況下收集了兒童的個人資料，會立即刪除。

7. 你的權利

依中華民國《個人資料保護法》及其他適用的法律，你可以要求查詢、閱覽、更正或刪除你的個人資料。請透過下方聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

8. 政策變更

如果本政策有重大變更，我們會在 app 內或本頁面公告，並更新上方的生效日期。

Refocus Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Refocus (the "app") is developed and operated by 王岳仁 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app accesses, collects, uses and shares, and how you can control it.

1. Data we access and collect

Accessibility Service (screen content)

The app uses Android's AccessibilityService to recognize when you are viewing short-form video screens such as YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels, and to automatically leave those screens during the hours you set.

  • It only runs while a supported app (such as YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok) is open.
  • It reads structural information about the screen, such as view identifiers, screen names, button text labels and accessibility descriptions, to decide whether the current screen is a short-video screen.
  • This information is processed in real time on your device and discarded immediately. It is never stored, uploaded to any server, or shared with anyone. We do not intentionally read or keep your message contents, passwords or other text you type.
  • Each time a short video is blocked, the app records only the date and the name of the blocked app, on your device, to show you statistics.

Usage Access (screen time)

If you grant Usage Access, the app reads your daily screen time to show this week's usage on the home screen. This is calculated and shown on your device only and is never uploaded.

Settings you enter

This includes your language, your estimate of daily phone use, your goals and your blocking schedule. By default these stay on your device. If you sign in, your schedule and goals are backed up to the cloud (see "Account" below).

Account (optional)

You can choose to sign in with Google or with email. When you sign in, we use Google Firebase to obtain and store your email address, a user identifier and, for Google sign-in, your name. After you sign in, your schedule and goals are stored in Google Firebase Firestore so you can restore them on a new phone. All features work without signing in.

Payments and subscriptions

Payments for the Pro subscription are handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive your subscription status (such as whether it is active, the plan and a purchase token). We never receive your card or other payment details.

Notifications

The app may send a notification to remind you if the Accessibility Service is turned off. The notification permission is not used to collect any data.

2. How we use data

  • To recognize and block short-video screens.
  • To show your screen time and blocking statistics.
  • To back up and restore your settings when you are signed in.
  • To check your subscription status and provide Pro features.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data. The app currently contains no advertising or third-party tracking tools.

3. Data sharing

We share data only with providers of services the app needs:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and cloud backup)
  • Google Play (subscription payments)

We do not give your data to any other third party unless required by law.

4. Security

Data sent to the cloud is encrypted with HTTPS, and access rules ensure only your own account can read or write your data.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Cloud data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account: delete it in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account, or request deletion by email as described on the account deletion page. We delete your account and all related cloud data within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Please cancel it separately in Google Play.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13. If you are a minor, please use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal data without consent, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable laws, you may ask to access, review, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will announce them in the app or on this page and update the effective date above.
